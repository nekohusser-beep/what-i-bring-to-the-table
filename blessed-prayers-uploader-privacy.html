BP Uploader: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

BP Uploader is a private tool run by Cord of Three Strands LLC (Denekio Husser) for one purpose: uploading and scheduling videos to our own YouTube channel, Blessed Prayers. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

Only our own YouTube channel, through the YouTube upload permission, and only to upload and update our own videos.

What it stores

An authorization token for our own channel, kept as a private secret in our own code repository settings. We do not collect, sell, share or store any other person's data.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
