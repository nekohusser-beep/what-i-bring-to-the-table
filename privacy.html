Privacy

What I Bring to the Table is made by Cord of Three Strands LLC.

Your data stays on your phone. Pantry selections, favorites, weekly plans, grocery checkmarks, and the date you first opened the app are saved in your browser's local storage only. We do not run user accounts, and this app does not send your pantry or recipes to a server.

If you use Export, you choose where that file goes. Share uses your device's own share sheet or clipboard.

Nutrition numbers are estimates from USDA FoodData Central and are not medical advice.
